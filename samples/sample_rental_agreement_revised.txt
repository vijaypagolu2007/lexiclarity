RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement is made on the 1st day of October 2026 between Mr. Suresh Rao ("Landlord"), residing at Hyderabad, Telangana, and Ms. Riya Sharma ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES. The Landlord hereby lets to the Tenant the residential flat bearing No. 3-45, Lotus Enclave, Madhapur, Hyderabad ("Premises"), together with fixtures and fittings listed in Annexure A.

2. TERM. The tenancy shall commence on 1 October 2026 and continue for a period of eleven (11) months, expiring on 31 August 2027, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement. The Agreement shall stand automatically renewed for successive periods of eleven months unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to expiry.

3. RENT. The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 19,500 (Rupees Nineteen Thousand Five Hundred only), payable in advance on or before the 5th day of each calendar month. The rent shall be subject to an escalation of 10% (ten percent) upon each renewal.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT. The Tenant shall deposit with the Landlord an interest-free refundable security deposit of Rs. 1,17,000 (Rupees One Lakh Seventeen Thousand only), refundable within ninety (90) days of vacation of the Premises, after deducting any amounts due for unpaid rent, damages beyond normal wear and tear, or outstanding utility charges.

5. LATE PAYMENT. In the event of delay in payment of rent beyond the 10th day of the month, the Tenant shall be liable to pay a late fee calculated at 10% per month on the outstanding amount, compounded monthly.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. The Tenant shall keep the Premises in good order and shall be responsible for all minor repairs up to Rs. 5,000 per incident. Structural repairs shall be the responsibility of the Landlord. The Tenant shall not make any structural alterations without the prior written consent of the Landlord.

7. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving one (1) month's prior written notice. However, the Landlord may terminate with only fifteen (15) days' notice in the event the Premises are required for personal use or sale. In the event the Tenant terminates within the first six months, the Tenant shall pay a lock-in penalty equal to two months' rent.

8. INDEMNITY. The Tenant shall indemnify and hold harmless the Landlord from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, and expenses, including legal fees, arising out of or in connection with the Tenant's use or occupation of the Premises, without any limitation as to amount.

9. INSPECTION. The Landlord or his agents may enter and inspect the Premises at any reasonable time with 24 hours' prior notice, or without notice in case of emergency.

10. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and the courts at Hyderabad shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement on the day and year first hereinabove written.

LANDLORD: Suresh Rao          TENANT: Riya Sharma
